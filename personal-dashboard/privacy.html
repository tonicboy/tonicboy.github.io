Personal Dashboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Personal Dashboard is a Chrome extension built for personal use. It has no servers of its own, and the developer never receives any of your data. This policy explains what the extension stores and where.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves your bookmarks in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. That includes titles, URLs, categories, and any icons or thumbnails you upload. It also saves your view preference and a record of its last Google Drive sync. None of this leaves your device unless you use Google Drive sync or export a file yourself.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync only starts after you click Sync and give access through Google's sign-in screen. The extension requests a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. That permission only reaches files this extension created.

  • The extension reads and writes one file in your Google Drive, personal-dashboard-bookmarks.json, which holds your bookmarks.
  • It can't see, read, or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your browser talks to Google's APIs directly. Your data isn't sent to the developer or to any other third party.
  • Your Google data is used only to store and fetch your bookmarks. It isn't sold, isn't used for advertising, and isn't read by anyone.

Personal Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Site-specific scripts

The extension includes small scripts that add features, such as keyboard shortcuts, to particular websites. They run only in your browser. They don't collect or send any data.

Analytics and tracking

The extension contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising code.

Deleting your data

  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes the data stored on your device. You can also delete individual bookmarks in the dashboard.
  • To remove the synced copy, delete personal-dashboard-bookmarks.json from your Google Drive.
  • To revoke the extension's Google access, go to your Google Account's third-party access page.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change with it.
